# ROGSCO
Resilient Online Graph-based Supply Chain Optimization under real world disruptive constraints

VISION:
Supply Chain Systems operate as large-scale dynamic networks involving suppliers, manufacturers, warehouses, distribution centres, retailers and customers, yet many existing approaches rely on static optimization, limited propagation modeling, and infrequent replanning. 

Real-time disruptions arise in the form of supplier failures, transportation bottlenecks, short- or long-term geopolitical instability and demand shocks that can propagate throughout the network and lead to significant logistical and operational impacts. 

ROGSCO seeks to enhance resilience and operational performance in dynamically evolving supply chains by reimagining the network as a fundamentally resilience-aware, online-optimization-driven, graph-theoretic, decision-making framework capable of adapting to real-time disruptions and uncertainty. In short, ROGSCO models supply chains as evolving graph structures and continuously re-optimizes resource allocation in response to disruption propagation.
